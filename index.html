About this map

Each symbol is a community water facility recorded in a field survey. Shape shows the technology. Green is working, amber is not working, red is abandoned and grey is under construction.

Status is what was recorded on the survey date and may have changed since. Ranges and nearby counts use straight-line distances. Real paths are usually longer.

Household tube wells are not included. N/A means the information was not available.
